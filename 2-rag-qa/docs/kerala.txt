Kerala is a state on the southwestern Malabar Coast of India. Its capital is Thiruvananthapuram. The state is known for its backwaters, especially in Alappuzha and Kumarakom, where visitors travel on traditional houseboats called kettuvallams. Kottayam is a district in central Kerala known for rubber plantations, publishing houses and the Vembanad lake. Kerala has one of the highest literacy rates in India. Onam is the most important festival of the state and is celebrated with a feast called Onasadya served on banana leaves.
